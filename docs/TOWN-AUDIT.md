# Town audit (offline, from a headless-server world)

Generated by `tools/qa/render_town.py` + `tools/qa/town_checks.py` on a saved world (seed `Nirn`, 22 forceloaded
areas, two server boots: boot 1 generated the chunks, boot 2 let the road planner drain its queue and paved the road
chunks around the samples). No client was started. Sheets: `docs/screens/town-audit/<place>.jpg`; road corridors:
`corridor_whiterun_riverwood.jpg`, `corridor_chorrol_skingrad.jpg`. Full-size outputs (plans, iso close-ups,
density charts, `report.json` per town) are in `tools/qa/out/town_audit/` (gitignored).

Reproduce: `py -3 -I tools/qa/town_checks.py <world> <samples.json>` and
`py -3 -I tools/qa/render_town.py <world> <place_id> <out_dir>` (`... corridor <idA> <idB> <out_dir>`).
Each sheet: plan view (2 px/block; buildings by wealth tier, streets, roads by class, props green, stalls magenta,
gates yellow), market-street / poor-quarter / gate isometric close-ups, clutter-density chart.

## 1. How towns are built (where clutter is placed)

* `settlement/plan/Cities.java`, `Layouts.java` + `grammar/Zoning.java` plan a settlement into `Spec`s (buildings,
  `STREET` lines, `GATE`, `WALL`, `YARD`, `STALL`, `GARDEN`, `PEN`); `Wealth` (POOR..NOBLE) is chosen per zone in
  `Zoning` (noble/centre/craft/outskirt zones, around line 536).
* Street furniture: `Zoning.lining()` (~line 940): lamp posts/braziers every 10-17 blocks on streets of rank <= 1 (only
  when `tier >= 1`), barrels/woodpiles/signposts on lesser streets, `1 + tier` cart/vendor/bench per square, wayside
  shrine/signpost at lane junctions with 1/3 chance, `2 + 2*tier` kennels/coops by random buildings, a dummy by
  barracks. Market stalls: `Zoning.square()` (2/5/8 by tier, main square only).
* Backyards: `Zoning.backyards()`: one 5x5 slot behind every home/inn/tannery/butcher: wealthy -> 50% garden, else
  flower bed or tree; poor/common -> garden, pen, laundry, woodpile or coop (roll of 6).
* Gates: `Zoning` wall pass (~line 1130): gates = skeleton road ends; unwalled towns get a signpost + brazier at each.
* Drawing: `grammar/Outdoors.yard()` (22 yard kinds, `gset` puts them on the real ground of their column),
  `Outdoors.pen/camp/quarry` for animals and work grounds. `build/Props.java` is INDOOR furniture/stations only.
* Roads: `roads/RoadGraph` plans polylines between places (`endpoint()` = the road's end at a capital/city),
  `RoadPlanner` A* with bridges, `Paver` lays blocks chunk-by-chunk (never inside structure pieces), `RoadLayout`
  places lanterns/signposts/milestones, `Wayshrines` picks the Divine, `InnSites` is the inn-site dev exporter.
* Nothing places clutter outside the wall except `RoadLayout` (lantern posts, signposts, milestones, shrines) and
  the outlying camps (`Outdoors.camp`).

## 2. Samples (21 places, all generated)

Skyrim capitals: whiterun, solitude, windhelm, riften, markarth, falkreath, morthal, dawnstar, winterhold.
Generic towns: chorrol (Imperial city), gnisis (Dunmer), mathiisen (Altmer), eagle_vale (Bosmer), alcaire (Breton),
kairou (Redguard), dushnikh_yal (Orc village), seaplace (Khajiit), arnesia (Argonian). Hamlet: stonehills. Port:
port_telvannis. Extra: riverwood (whiterun <-> riverwood corridor). Footprints are <= 400x400 (place radius + 30, max 200).
Haiku scoring was run on all 21 (two reviewers, 11 + 10 sheets, not 7 + 7).

## 4. Worst five

1. **dushnikh_yal (Orc village, 2.0).** A 40-block palisade ring around one longhouse and one hut (2 buildings,
   6 props, one gate). Five roads end at the centre and cross the palisade where there is no gate (see its sheet);
   most of the ring is empty ground. Needs a stronghold layout with its own yard set (forge, pens, hides, fires).
2. **falkreath (Nord capital, 2.1).** Reads as a hamlet: 16 buildings, no wall, no gate (so no road join at all), 27
   orphaned log fragments from tree clearing, 2 floating props, lamp posts planned but missing. Haiku: roads pass the
   town and "end in forest".
3. **solitude (Nord capital, 2.7 but worst automated).** 0.81 props/building (lowest), 14 of its planned lamp
   posts/braziers have no blocks, 48 stray log blocks, the only gate is 67 blocks from the nearest road end, and
   density is *flat* (0.0 inner / 0.1 outer): the capital of Haafingar has almost no clutter.
4. **eagle_vale (Bosmer town, 2.4).** Gate is 24.8 blocks from the road end, props sit on the road and street
   (4 + 2), 5 planned props not built, a floating woodpile, and Haiku "no gate visible, SW road stops short".
5. **winterhold (Nord capital, 2.3).** Every house is the same type, no gate, 19 props over 16 buildings; roads cross
   open ice. Close runners-up: **riverwood** (3 gates, 1 within 24 blocks of a road; props on the highway; 13 stray
   logs) and **markarth** (0.5 props per wealthy building, incomplete wall).

## 5. Common failures

* **Roads stop short of capital/city gates (and only one of several gates is ever served).** `RoadGraph.endpoint()`
  ends a road at `centre + (max(place radius, Cities.reach) + 8)` in the "main gate" direction, but the grammar plan's
  wall/gate sits at the built radius. Chorrol: road end 158 blocks from centre, gate ~68 -> a 90-block unpaved gap
  (paver does not pave inside structure pieces and the town has no approach street outside the gate). Solitude 67
  and Windhelm 73 blocks. Towns/villages end roads at the centre instead and the road crosses the wall wherever it
  happens to arrive (dushnikh_yal, kairou, riverwood), not at a gate. 12 of 26 gate points have no road within 24.
* **Clutter exists only inside the wall.** Density falls from 8.8 props/1000 m2 (inner third, mean over 18 towns) to
  0.12 (outer third). In 11 of 21 towns no prop lies beyond 50 m of the centre, in 17 of 21 none beyond 75 m (the
  farthest in any town is ~125 m; whiterun's built radius is 146 but its last prop is at 100). Outer roads carry only
  `RoadLayout` lanterns/signposts/shrines; no carts, barrels, hay, fences.
* **Capitals are the least cluttered places.** Nord capitals: 0.8-1.6 props per building, generic towns 1.4-3.0
  (eagle_vale 2.8, port_telvannis 2.7). Counts in `Zoning.lining()` are per-tier constants, not scaled by area or
  building count; stalls only 2/5/8 in the main square.
* **Wealth tiers are not visible in props.** Weighted props per building: poor 1.59, common 1.24, wealthy 1.74,
  noble 0.90. Noble houses have the fewest; poor areas get no distinct set (no rubbish, lean-tos, hay, laundry
  lines concentrated); Haiku scored wealth 2-3 almost everywhere.
* **Planned props that were not built.** 44 specs (mostly `LAMP_POST`, `BRAZIER`, `FOUNTAIN`, `NOTICE_BOARD`, plus
  barrels/woodpile in Bosmer/Dunmer towns) left no block at their column: whiterun 13, solitude 14. Probably painted
  over by a later street/terrace piece, or dropped at chunk seams; not investigated in code.
* **Roads route straight through yards.** 56 props lie within a road polyline's width (alcaire 11, kairou 8, stonehills
  8). The paver skips structure pieces so nothing is paved over them, but the road then simply ends at the town edge.
* **Orphaned tree logs / fronds.** 303 one-block fragments; whiterun 49, solitude 48, windhelm 59, riften 36, falkreath 27.
  Origin is the clearing/trimming near buildings (`Outdoors.CLEARING`, `Canvas` terrain trim) cutting trees.
* **Dead road ends:** only Arnesia (5 roads end at its centre in water; the town sits on a river).
* **Wayside:** corridor maps show junctions with bridges, shrines and signposts at sensible spacing; Haiku saw "no
  wayshrines" on the whiterun-riverwood stretch (Nord lands only get Divine shrines on highways), and trail spurs
  ending in settlement boxes around Chorrol.

## 6. Ranked fix list

1. Road-to-gate join (`roads/RoadGraph.endpoint`, `roads/RoadNetwork.end`, `settlement/grammar/Zoning` gate pass):
   end capital/city roads at the actual plan gate (plan `x.sk.gates`, or `Zoning.builtRadius(size, radius)` + gate
   offset) instead of `radius + 8`; add a paved approach (a `STREET`/path `Spec` outside each served gate); for
   towns/villages route the road to a gate, not the centre.
2. Gates without a road (`Zoning` wall pass): only keep gates that a road/lane serves, or have `RoadPlanner` add a
   lane from every extra gate to the nearest road (Chorrol W/E, Kairou, Riverwood, Gnisis).
3. Outskirt clutter (`Zoning.lining`, `Outdoors.yard`, `roads/RoadLayout`): scale yard counts with built area, add an
   outskirts band (50-100 m: carts, hay, crates, troughs, fenced paddocks, woodpiles) and a sparse road-side set
   (odd cart, barrel, signpost) along approach roads; this fixes the inner/outer 8.8 -> 0.1 cliff.
4. Capital density (`Zoning.lining`, `Zoning.square`): `carts = 1 + tier`, `pets = 2 + 2*tier`, 8 stalls max are
   flat; scale by building count so whiterun/solitude/windhelm reach >= 1.5 props per building.
5. Wealth identity (`Zoning.backyards`, `Wealth`, `Outdoors`): noble set (fountain, statue, hedges, braziers,
   banners), common set (stalls, barrels, signs), poor set (laundry lines, hay, rubbish, lean-tos, more props per
   building near the wall); currently poor ~ wealthy and noble is lowest.
6. Missing props (`Outdoors.yard` LAMP_POST/BRAZIER/FOUNTAIN, `Canvas` paint order): find why ~25% of planned
   lamp posts leave no block (street/terrace painted after yards; chunk seam), whiterun/solitude first.
7. Stray logs (`Outdoors.CLEARING`, `Canvas` clearing): also remove log/leaf remnants after clearing (the 303 blocks).
8. Orc strongholds and small capitals (`settlement/plan/Cities`, `Layouts`): dushnikh_yal has 2 buildings in a 40-block
   ring; Falkreath/Winterhold/Dawnstar are 14-16 buildings with no wall or gate.
9. Roads through yards (`RoadPlanner`/`Paver`): treat structure footprints as obstacles inside the box so polylines
   do not run through yard specs.
10. Arnesia (`RoadGraph.endpoint`/`Landmass`): centre is in water; pick a dry endpoint like the capital branch does.

## 7. Check definitions (`tools/qa/town_checks.py`)

* gate road: nearest road polyline vertex-segment within 24 blocks of a `GATE` piece (unwalled towns: the major
  signposts); `gates_without_street_24` also reported (internal street end within 24).
* road end: an end of a road touching the box must lie in a settlement box (+24) or within 40 of a settlement or within
  6 of another road, and not on water.
* props vs roads/buildings: YARD/STALL centre vs `STREET` width, vs road polyline width by class (5/4/3/2), vs building
  footprints.
* density: props per 1000 m2 in 25 m rings from the place centre; per-wealth props within 9 blocks of buildings.
* presence: a prop column must rise above the median of the ring around it or have a non-terrain top block.
* floating: lowest built run of a prop column with >= 2 air cells below and no horizontal neighbour; plus stray blocks
  anywhere (non-terrain block, top of column, six neighbours empty).
