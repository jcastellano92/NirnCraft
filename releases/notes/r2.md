# Release r2

## Sky and shaders
- **Mountain-shadowed sunrise and sunset.** The mod now measures how high the terrain stands toward the sun (east when the
  sun is rising, west when setting) from the world map out to 14,000 blocks and gives the shader pack that angle. With the
  patched pack the sun dims, reddens and fades as it drops behind a far ridge, and comes back (with the usual sunset glow)
  as it clears it. Valleys do not dim the noon sun. Local shade from nearby hills and buildings still comes from the
  shader's own shadow map. Needs the optional patched shader pack in this zip.
- Storm towers and rain curtains are now spread over neighbouring 64-block cells, so a lone cell can no longer show as a
  single vertical white column in the shader (this is the likeliest cause of the "white lines" in your screenshots).
- (r1) weather blends across region borders; new regions grow in instead of popping; rain turns to snow gradually;
  lowland snow lines raised.
