# Release r4 (settlements and quests)

- **Temples:** a settlement now keeps one temple and one hall of the dead. The layout, the zoning wants and the
  "whatever planning left out" pass could each ask for one, giving cities several. Extras become ordinary houses on the
  same lot; named or fixed buildings from city plan files are never touched.
- **Farms in town:** kitchen gardens, pens and orchards stay out of the built-up core. For towns and cities the core is
  now 80% of the settlement radius (was 65%), so crop plots no longer sit beside market stalls.
- **Quest creatures on roofs:** quest spawns no longer use a building's roof, awning, beam or fence as "ground", so
  skeevers (and other targets) are no longer placed on top of someone's house.

Includes everything from r1-r3 and the sun-behind-mountains shader.
