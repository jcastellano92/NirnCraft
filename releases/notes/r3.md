# Release r3 (quick gameplay fixes)

- **Waiting:** "You cannot wait with enemies nearby" no longer triggers for hostiles that never noticed you. Only hostiles
  hunting or searching for you block it; an unaware hostile blocks only when within about 6 blocks with a clear line.
- **Fast travel in multiplayer:** travelling now advances the clock with other players online too (config
  `world.travelTimeMultiplayer`, default on; turn it off to keep other players' clocks from jumping).
- **Repeating arrival banner:** the region arrival banner no longer replays on every world load or respawn while only one
  region has been entered.
- **Bounty line vs enemy health bar:** the bounty text now drops below the enemy bar while one is showing, instead of
  being drawn on top of it.
- **Jails:** cell doors and the cell-block gate are locked by default (adept lock: needs a jail key or a lockpick), not only
  while a sentence is being served.
- Shopkeepers keep a free row behind the counter (r1); weather/sky fixes (r1, r2); sun behind mountains (r2).
