# Release r1

## Weather and sky
- Cloud/weather field now blends neighbouring regions across ~400 blocks, so weather no longer changes at a hard region line.
- A region that comes into range while others are known now grows out of the surrounding sky instead of popping in fully formed (login and teleport still start formed).
- Precipitation shafts/curtains fade out under thin cloud, which removes the stray white vertical streak in a fair sky.
- Rain turns to snow gradually over a 60-block band around the snow line (sleet), so a town on a slope no longer gets rain in one part and snow in another.
- Lowland snow lines raised (Whiterun, Falkreath, Rift, Reach, generic Skyrim temperate: 190-210 to 240-250), so Whiterun's plains stop getting snow.
