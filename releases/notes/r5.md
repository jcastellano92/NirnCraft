# Release r5 (verified with real in-game screenshots)

- **Snow in Whiterun fixed.** Whiterun's biome mix included the snowy "pale heath" (a border blend toward the Pale that applied
  to the whole hold), which put snow patches inside the city and on its plains. Removed at the source (`tools/worldgen/ecotones.py`);
  confirmed in-game: the streets are bare ground.
- **Big bare grey/dirt expanse outside Whiterun's gate fixed.** Skyrim Highlands (the foothills around Whiterun) used rock/gravel/
  coarse-dirt thresholds that left over half the hill country bare; they now match the neighbouring tundra's gentle share
  (`S_HIGHLANDS` in `tools/worldgen/biomes.py`, regenerated). Confirmed in-game: the expanse is grass.
- **Bees:** hives placed by the world (apiaries, farm hives) now get honey bees moved in once, and vanilla bees take over
  (leave by day, pollinate flowers, return, fill the hive). Player-placed hives are left alone.
- **Honey is local produce:** a place keeps bees by one deterministic rule shared by the planner and the economy. Where it
  does, the settlement builds an apiary, honey/honeycomb are among its exports, and its inn sells them. Places without hives
  have no honey.
- **Visual test tooling:** `tools/qa/vis_tour.ps1` runs the real client through a teleport tour and saves screenshots;
  `tools/qa/prism_autotest.ps1` does it in a throw-away Prism instance with the real shader pack. The tour skips the opening
  cottage first (it was holding the camera).
- Includes r1-r4.
